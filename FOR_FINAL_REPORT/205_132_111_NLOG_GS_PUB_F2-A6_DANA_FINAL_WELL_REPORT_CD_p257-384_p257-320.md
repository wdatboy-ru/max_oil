### 6. Трещины и разломы

From 625m onwards the flow had to be reduced again due to the losses. At 685m it was called TD for this section... Total losses were observed once the assembly was pulled into the conductor shoe... set cement plugs over the loss zone at approx. 500m") — скважина F/02-A06 (AWB), секция 26", пласты Top Maassluis / Top Oosterhout, интервал 500–685 m MD, дата 19-Jul-2013 [стр. 44, 45](<file:///D:/Argon_Jobs/Hanze_docs/parts/132_111_NLOG_GS_PUB_F2-A6_DANA_FINAL_WELL_REPORT_CD_p257-384_p257-320.pdf#page=44>).
